# ChefPulse Refund & Cancellation Policy

**Version:** 1.0  
**Business name:** ChefPulse

## 1. Chef cancels
If a Chef cancels a confirmed ChefPulse booking, the customer is eligible for a **full refund of the applicable ChefPulse service amount collected, including the applicable ₹49 platform/surge fee**, subject to applicable law and payment-provider processing requirements.

## 2. Customer cancels
Customers may cancel while the booking remains cancellable in the ChefPulse app.

**No cancellation is available once the Chef has reached the customer's service address.**

If the customer cancels before the Chef reaches the service address and the cancellation qualifies under the applicable booking rules, ChefPulse will issue the applicable refund of the ChefPulse service amount collected, including the applicable ₹49 platform/surge fee, subject to applicable law and payment-provider processing requirements.

## 3. Grocery purchases
Groceries purchased through **Zepto, Blinkit or BigBasket** are legally sold and fulfilled by the applicable grocery partner. ChefPulse is not the grocery seller and does not promise or process grocery refunds on the partner's behalf.

Grocery cancellations, substitutions, missing items, damaged items, delivery problems and refunds are governed by the applicable grocery partner's policies and applicable law.

## 4. Refund processing
Approved ChefPulse refunds are sent through the payment method and payment provider used for the transaction. Processing times may depend on the payment provider, bank or card issuer.
