# ChefPulse Terms & Conditions

**Version:** 1.0  
**Business name:** ChefPulse

## 1. About ChefPulse
ChefPulse is an on-demand marketplace that helps customers request and receive chef services at their chosen address. ChefPulse facilitates the booking, payment and service relationship between customers and chefs.

## 2. Chef Now service
Customers may request an available chef through the Chef Now flow. Chef availability, acceptance and estimated arrival information shown in the app may change based on real-time availability and operational conditions.

A booking becomes confirmed when the assigned chef accepts the request. Customers must provide accurate contact and service-address information and remain available to receive the chef.

## 3. Payments and platform fee
ChefPulse collects payments for ChefPulse chef-service bookings through the payment methods made available in the app. Customers must not pay the Chef directly for the ChefPulse service unless ChefPulse expressly instructs otherwise.

A **₹49 platform/surge fee per order** may apply. The applicable amount and any applicable taxes will be shown before payment.

## 4. Ingredients and grocery delivery
After a chef accepts a booking, the chef may provide an ingredient list. Customers may purchase ingredients through grocery partners made available in ChefPulse, including **Zepto, Blinkit and BigBasket**.

ChefPulse is **not the seller of those groceries** and does not control grocery inventory, substitutions, delivery operations or grocery-partner refunds. Grocery purchases are subject to the applicable grocery partner's own terms, conditions, pricing and refund policies.

ChefPulse may display grocery-delivery status in the app, including a distinct status confirming that groceries have been delivered to the customer's service address.

## 5. Customer cancellation
A customer may cancel a booking while the booking remains cancellable in the app.

**Cancellation is no longer available once the Chef has reached the customer's service address.**

Where a customer cancellation qualifies for a refund under the applicable cancellation rules, ChefPulse will refund the applicable ChefPulse service amount collected, including the applicable ₹49 platform/surge fee, subject to applicable law and payment-provider processing requirements.

## 6. Chef cancellation
If the Chef cancels a confirmed booking, ChefPulse will provide a **full refund of the applicable ChefPulse service amount collected, including the applicable ₹49 platform/surge fee**, subject to applicable law and payment-provider processing requirements.

## 7. Chef arrival and service
Once the Chef reaches the customer's service address, the cancellation window closes. Customers should provide safe and reasonable access to the service location and communicate any relevant cooking requirements, allergies or dietary restrictions before service begins.

ChefPulse does not guarantee that every requested chef, cuisine, time or service will always be available.
